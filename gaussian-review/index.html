bridge to ML

The covariance matrix is the shape manual.

For two features, the diagonal tells how much each feature spreads. The off-diagonal tells whether they move together.

Var(X)horizontal spread
Cov(X,Y)tilt
Cov(Y,X)same tilt
Var(Y)vertical spread

Quick read: positive off-diagonal → right-up. Negative off-diagonal → right-down. Zeros → no linear tilt.

3.2 · multivariate Gaussian

One model for a whole cloud.

μ puts the cloud’s center somewhere. Σ decides whether that cloud is a circle, an upright ellipse, or a tilted ellipse.

Spherical: same spread in every direction.
Diagonal: different spreads, but no tilt.
Full: different spreads and a tilt.
x ~ N( μ, Σ )   →   center + shape
conditional Gaussian

Evidence moves the prediction — and narrows it.

Assume X and Y are standardized (both have mean 0 and standard deviation 1). If they correlate, observing Y gives real information about X.

Key idea: the conditional mean becomes ρ × Y, while the conditional variance becomes 1 − ρ².

Prediction for X0.80
Remaining variance0.36

Because ρ = 0.80 and Y = 1.00, the best prediction shifts from 0 to 0.80. The uncertainty falls from 1.00 to 0.36.
