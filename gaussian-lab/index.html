What the controls mean

Variance sits on the diagonal: σ₁² and σ₂² control horizontal and vertical spread. Covariance sits off the diagonal: Cov(X,Y)=ρσ₁σ₂ and controls shared direction. Correlation divides covariance by both standard deviations, so it stays between −1 and 1.

For a multivariate Gaussian, μ locates the center and Σ determines the contours. Full covariance gives tilted ellipses; diagonal covariance gives axis-aligned ellipses; spherical covariance gives circles.
